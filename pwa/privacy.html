Privacy policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Nuthan Weather does not require an account and does not operate an advertising or analytics service. The app developer does not receive weather searches, device location, or chat messages through a private app server.

Weather and location

You can search for a place or optionally tap the location button and grant permission. The app sends the selected place or coordinates to Open-Meteo to return weather and forecast data. Location is not collected in the background. The PWA saves the selected place and temperature-unit preference locally in your browser.

Optional AI chat

AI chat is optional. To use it, you provide your own Gemini API key. The app holds the key in memory for the current session, does not save it to device storage, and sends it with your prompts and chat history directly to Google's Gemini API. Google's processing of this information is subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy policy. Do not send sensitive information in chat.

Retention and control

The app developer does not receive or retain the weather requests, precise location, Gemini key, or chat history on an app-operated server. You can clear saved app data in your browser or Android settings. Closing/restarting the app clears the in-memory Gemini key and chat session.

Children and questions

The app is a general-purpose weather utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children. For privacy questions, contact the maintainer through the project support page.

External services

Weather: Open-Meteo terms. AI chat: Google privacy policy and Gemini API terms. Those external services have their own privacy practices.
